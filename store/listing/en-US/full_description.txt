Prosoche gives you a few quiet minutes with the Quran each day. Then you're done.

No account. No ads. Your prayers, reading and progress stay on your phone.

DAILY CARDS THAT END
Each day you get a short set of cards: verses of the Quran with a translation of their meaning and a hadith. The set has a clear end, about 5 minutes. There is no endless feed and nothing plays on its own.

READ THE QURAN
• The full Arabic text (Tanzil, Uthmani script), shown without changes
• Translations of the meanings in English, Indonesian, Urdu and Turkish
• Search by word, surah name or reference such as 2:255
• Prosoche remembers where you stopped

PRAYER TIMES AND QIBLA
• Prayer times are calculated on your phone, also offline
• Choose the calculation method, Asr time and high latitude rule, or adjust a time to match your local mosque
• A quiet reminder at each prayer time and one in the morning, if you want them
• Private mode shows only the app name and time on the lock screen
• Qibla compass

YOUR JOURNEY, KEPT PRIVATE
• Mark prayers as on time, late, missed or made up
• A private streak with grace days, or hide all counters with Simple mode
• Save cards you want to come back to
• See which surahs you have read
• Islamic calendar with an adjustable Hijri date

CALM BY DESIGN
No points, no leaderboards, no guilt notifications, no red badges. Light and dark theme.

PRIVACY
• Works without an account
• Your location is used only on your phone to calculate prayer times. You can choose a city instead.
• Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are off by default. You can turn them on in Settings, and turn them off at any time.
• "Delete all my data" in Settings removes everything from your phone.

SOURCES
Arabic Quran text: Tanzil Project (tanzil.net). Translations of the meanings: QuranEnc.com (Saheeh International / Noor International Center; Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs; Muhammad Ibrahim Junagarhi; Rowwad Translation Center). Hadith: HadeethEnc.com. City data: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Every card shows its source, and the "Sources & licenses" screen lists versions and licenses.

PLEASE NOTE
Translations convey the meanings of the Quran; they are not the Quran itself. Prayer times can differ from your local mosque by several minutes; please confirm them locally. Reminders may be delayed by Android or battery settings. Prosoche is a reminder and habit tool and does not issue religious rulings.
